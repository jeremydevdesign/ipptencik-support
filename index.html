Common questions

Where do the scores come from?

From the official IPPT scoring tables published on the OneNS website, for push-ups, sit-ups and the 2.4 km run across every age group. Scores are for your information only; your official result is the one recorded at your IPPT session.

Is this an official MINDEF or SAF app?

No. IPPT Encik is an independent app. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with MINDEF, the SAF or any government agency.

Which age does the app use?

Your IPPT age goes up on your birthday, not on 1 January. If you enter your date of birth in Profile, the app works this out for you, and scores each logged test with your age on that test's date. You can also set your age by hand instead.

Why is my pass mark different from my friend's?

NSmen pass at 51 points. NSFs and Regulars need 61 points. Set your service status in Profile. The incentive amounts shown ($200, $300, $500) apply to NSmen only.

Are female and other scoring tables available?

Not yet. The app currently uses the male scoring tables. More will be added once they can be checked against an official source.

How do I keep my results if I change phones?

Your results are stored only on your phone. In Profile, tap Export backup and save the file somewhere safe, such as Files or iCloud Drive. On your new phone, install IPPT Encik and tap Restore to pick that file. Restoring only adds results; it never deletes any.

I bought Remove ads. How do I get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Go to Profile and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. The ads turn off again at no extra charge.

Why does the app ask to track me?

The free version shows ads from Google. iOS asks whether you are happy for those ads to be tailored to you. Either answer works, and you still see ads either way. Your IPPT results are never shared. See the privacy policy for details.
