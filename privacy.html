IPPT Encik

Privacy policy

The short version: your IPPT results, age and profile stay on your phone. The app shows ads from Google, and if you buy Remove ads, the purchase is handled by RevenueCat.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

IPPT Encik ("the app") is an IPPT score calculator and logbook for iPhone, made in Singapore. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server of ours that holds your results.

IPPT Encik is an independent app. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with MINDEF, the SAF or any government agency.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter into IPPT Encik - your date of birth or age, service status, vocation, logged test results, notes and settings - is stored locally on your device only, in the app's private storage.

We cannot see this data. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and it is not shared with the ad or purchase services described below.

If you delete IPPT Encik, this data is removed with it. We have no copy and cannot recover it for you, which is why the app lets you export a backup.

Backups you create yourself

IPPT Encik can export your results and profile to a backup file. This happens only when you choose to do it.

When you export, iOS hands the file to you through the standard share sheet, and you decide where it goes - for example Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or an email to yourself. Once you send it somewhere, that destination's own privacy policy applies to it. The app does not upload the file anywhere, and we never receive it.

Restoring a backup reads the file you pick and adds its results to the ones already on your phone.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of IPPT Encik shows ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, and occasionally a full-screen ad after you log a result. The ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads, Google's software in the app collects information about your device and how ads are used, such as:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (for example the country)
  • device identifiers - the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and otherwise identifiers limited to this app or developer
  • which ads were shown and whether they were tapped
  • technical information such as the device model, iOS version, app performance and crash reports

None of your IPPT results, age, date of birth or other entries are given to Google.

Tracking is your choice. The first time the app shows ads, iOS asks whether IPPT Encik may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, Google can use your advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. If you ask the app not to track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you still see ads, just less tailored ones. You can change this any time in the iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

The app asks Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Remove ads purchase (RevenueCat)

You can remove the ads with a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

To confirm and restore the purchase, the app uses a service called RevenueCat. When you open the Remove ads screen, buy, or tap Restore purchase, the app sends RevenueCat:

  • your purchase history for this app (which product was bought, when, and the App Store receipt that proves it)
  • a random, anonymous ID that the app creates for this purpose, so the purchase can be recognised again, for example after reinstalling
  • basic technical information such as the app version, iOS version, country or region of your App Store account, and whether you allowed tracking

The anonymous ID is not your name, email or Apple Account, and we do not link it to you. RevenueCat does not receive your IPPT results or profile. If you never open the Remove ads screen or tap Restore purchase, the app does not contact RevenueCat at all.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Other information

The App Store itself may collect information about downloads, purchases and crashes under Apple's own policies. That is between you and Apple.

If you email us, we will have your email address and whatever you choose to write to us. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the app. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Permissions

IPPT Encik asks for a permission only when it needs it: the tracking question described above, and access to Files when you export or restore a backup. It does not request contacts, location services, camera, photos, microphone or Health data.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: delete the app, or delete individual results in History. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
  • Advertising: you can reset or limit your advertising identifier and tracking in the iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security. Google's data choices are described in its privacy policy.
  • Purchase records: because the RevenueCat ID is anonymous, we cannot look up your record from your name or email. If you want it deleted, email us and we will help as far as we can.

Children

IPPT Encik is made for national servicemen and others preparing for the IPPT, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Your results are held in your device's app-private storage and are protected by your device passcode, Face ID or Touch ID and by iOS's own file protection. Because your results never leave your device, there is no server of ours holding them that could be breached. The trade-off is that we cannot restore them for you if you lose your phone, so please keep a backup.

Not official scoring

Scores in IPPT Encik are calculated from the official IPPT scoring tables published on the OneNS website, for your information only. Your official result is the one recorded at your IPPT session.
